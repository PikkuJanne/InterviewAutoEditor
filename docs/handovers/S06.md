## Changes and reasoning

- `interview_auto_editor/planner.py` implements the global union of speech,
  reaction, uncertainty, overlap and conservative turn barriers. Guards expand
  to at least 120 ms onset/200 ms tail without shrinking existing protection.
  Confident single-source-group speech boundaries select same/different rules;
  unknown/shared/reaction boundaries preserve gaps. No activity or no approval
  preserves the recording. Picture candidates do not affect removal decisions.
- Tight uses different-group threshold/residual 0.80/0.25 s and same-group
  1.50/0.50 s. Gentle uses 1.20/0.50 s and 2.50/0.90 s. Thresholds are strict;
  residual is at least combined actual protection. Default quarter-second
  targets therefore retain at least 0.32 s. These are pacing hypotheses, not
  validated preferences. Removal boundaries round inward to absolute frames.
- Strict `PlannerRequest`, `GapApproval`, `GapDecision` and `PlanningResult`
  retain complete source-bound evidence. Approval means an explicit all-track
  removable interval from construction labels or human review, bound to the
  complete analysis digest. A negative detector/noise-floor/correlation result
  is not an approval. Stale/unknown/out-of-domain contracts fail closed.
  Algorithm `s06-shared-pauses-1` fixes defaults/semantics; no implicit migration.
- Keep/audible overrides retain the entire source-time interval globally. S06
  creates no gain changes. Replanning Keep restores time without inference.
  The original analysis, sources, fingerprints, events/reviews/shots and exact
  timing evidence remain unchanged. Existing origin/tail refusals run first,
  even before an identity plan can be returned.
- `explain()` supplies exact rational source/output seconds, frame ranges,
  reasons, approval IDs, residual targets and output seams. Reviews retain source
  locations/reasons with mapped output times or null inside removed material.
  Output positions derive from retained integer frames, without float drift.
- `tools/s06_plan.py` writes a new private result atomically/exclusively. Exit 0
  means no reviews, 2 mapped reviews, 1 refusal/failure and 130 cancellation.
  Fixed errors omit private paths/content. S10 defines the integrated CLI.
- `tools/test_s06_planner.py` and `tests/fixtures/planner-v1/` add exact regressions
  and fixed v1 snapshots with independent construction-labelled gaps. These
  JSON/PCM fixtures are mathematical, **not Kdenlive-authored projects/media**,
  human word annotations or acoustic detector-accuracy evidence.
- `docs/project/S06_PLANNER.md`, README, contract/compatibility notes, aggregate
  evidence, STATE/NEXT_STEP and this handover document actual behavior and limits.

The explicit approval requirement is the material S06 decision: the event
complement cannot authorize cuts under the existing no-silence-certificate rule.
Real processing intentionally remains conservative until genuine global gap
evidence exists. No per-track removal, filler/rhetorical editing, voice identity,
source repair or new listening workflow was introduced. Shared seam boundaries
are present; meaningful turn/group construction and automatic export remain S07.
